AIML

An Explainable AI-based ML Framework With Fuzzy Logic for Early Detection of Chronic Kidney Disease

Designed and implemented an Explainable AI-based machine learning framework integrated with Fuzzy Logic for early detection of Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD). Applied multiple classification algorithms including Naive Bayes, Support Vector Machine, and Decision Tree to analyze clinical data and predict disease risk. Incorporated SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to enhance model interpretability by explaining feature contributions to predictions. Leveraged Fuzzy Logic to handle uncertainty and imprecision in medical parameters.
